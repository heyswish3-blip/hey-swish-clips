Hey Swish Clips

A local tool for creating short-form video clips — and publishing them to TikTok and YouTube accounts you connect yourself.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What it does

Hey Swish Clips helps a creator turn long videos, such as podcast episodes, into short vertical clips. It runs on your own computer and can:

  • transcribe spoken audio,
  • suggest short clip candidates with titles and captions,
  • render ready-to-post vertical video files with burned-in captions,
  • upload clips through official platform APIs only after you connect your own accounts and approve each step.

Your accounts, your control

Nothing is posted automatically to strangers, and nothing is posted without setup: publishing requires your own connected TikTok or YouTube account, your explicit approval during login, and your own API credentials stored locally on your machine. Test uploads stay private by default.

How publishing works

  • TikTok: the tool uses TikTok Login Kit to connect your account and the official Content Posting API to submit videos you approve.
  • YouTube: the tool uses Google OAuth to connect your channel and the official YouTube Data API to upload videos as private by default.
  • Login tokens and secrets are stored only in local configuration files on your own computer. They are never published, never committed to public code, and never shown publicly.

Hey Swish Clips is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed, or sponsored by TikTok, ByteDance, Google, YouTube, or Meta.
